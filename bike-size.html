Schwinn Volare 1500 — Large Frame

A rider who is 76 inches (6'4", ~193 cm) tall falls at the top of the Large frame range for the Schwinn Volare 1500. The Large frame is the biggest size offered in the Volare series and is rated for riders from approximately 6'1"–6'4".

The Volare 1500 is the only current Volare model whose frame range extends to 6'4". The Volare 1200 and Volare 1300 top out at 6'2" and are not recommended at that height.

Schwinn Volare Model Comparison

Recommended

Volare 1500

  • Height range: 5'2"–6'4"
  • Sizes: SM / MD / LG
  • Gears: Shimano 24-speed
  • Brakes: Mechanical disc
  • Tires: 700c × 32c
  • Best for: fitness, road, urban

Volare 1300

  • Height range: 5'8"–6'2"
  • Sizes: Medium only
  • Gears: Shimano 14-speed
  • Brakes: Caliper
  • Tires: 700c × 28c

Volare 1200

  • Height range: 5'4"–6'2"
  • Sizes: ~17" (Medium)
  • Gears: Shimano 21-speed
  • Brakes: Disc / rim
  • Tires: 700c
  • Best for: fitness, urban

Note: The Volare 1300 and 1200 only fit up to approximately 6'2" and are not recommended for a 76" (6'4") rider. The Volare 1500 Large is the correct choice.

General Bike Fitting Tips

Measurement How to check
Standover height Stand over the top tube — there should be 1–2" clearance between you and the tube.
Seat height With your foot on the pedal at the lowest point, your knee should have a slight bend (~25–35°).
Reach Arms should have a slight bend; no need to stretch uncomfortably to reach the handlebars.
Frame size formula Inseam (cm) × 0.67 = approximate road frame size in cm.
